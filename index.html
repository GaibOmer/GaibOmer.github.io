Hermes YouTube Yukleme

Bu uygulama, kanal sahibinin kendi YouTube kanallarina (Vaka Mercegi, Ehli Sunnet Sesli Kitap, Ahl al-Sunnah Audiobooks) kendi bilgisayarindan video yuklemesi icin kullandigi kisisel bir aractir. Ucuncu kisilere hizmet vermez.

Gizlilik Politikasi / Privacy Policy

Uygulama yalnizca YouTube Data API uzerinden kanal sahibinin yetki verdigi kanallara video, baslik, aciklama, kucuk resim ve oynatma listesi bilgisi yukler. Hicbir kullanici verisi toplanmaz, satilmaz, paylasilmaz veya ucuncu taraf sunuculara gonderilmez. Yetki belirteci (OAuth token) yalnizca kanal sahibinin kendi bilgisayarinda saklanir ve istenildigi an https://myaccount.google.com/permissions adresinden iptal edilebilir.

This app is a personal tool used only by the channel owner to upload videos to their own YouTube channels via the YouTube Data API. No user data is collected, sold, shared or sent to third-party servers. OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
